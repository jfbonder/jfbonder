# Julián Fernández Bonder

I'm a professor at the Department of Mathematics, Universidad de Buenos Aires (FCEN),
and Principal Researcher at CONICET, based at the Instituto de Cálculo.

My research focuses on nonlinear and nonlocal elliptic-type equations:
problems in Orlicz–Sobolev spaces, fractional operators, shape optimization,
and eigenvalue problems for quasilinear operators. Lately I have also been
working on numerical methods and PINNs for these problems.

**Website:** [https://mate.dm.uba.ar/~jfbonder/index.html](https://mate.dm.uba.ar/~jfbonder/index.html) — publications, teaching, and links to
Google Scholar, arXiv, MathSciNet, ORCID and zbMATH.

**Contact:** [jfbonder@dm.uba.ar](mailto:jfbonder@dm.uba.ar)

## Research code

- [pxlaplace](https://github.com/jfbonder/pxlaplace) — Python solver for the p(x)-Laplacian on general 2D domains (with A. Aragón and D. Rubio).
- [PINN-eigenvalues-v2](https://github.com/jfbonder/PINN-eigenvalues-v2) — PINNs for eigenvalues in elliptic problems (with A. Salort).
- [universe25-abm](https://github.com/jfbonder/universe25-abm) — agent-based model for the social collapse in Calhoun's Universe 25 experiment.
